Partition Wall Component

In progress
Grasshopper / componentsInterior subdivision

Overview

A reusable rectangle component for interior partition walls. Parametric control over length and thickness lets walls be dropped into a plan and adjusted in place. Pairs with the wall-thickness generator for full plan assemblies.

Parameters

  • Length — wall run, driven by slider or curve input
  • Thickness — section depth, slider-driven
